Our Mission

  Carolina Boxer Rescue is a refuge for boxers who find themselves abandoned. We promise them safety, wellness, and a second chance. CBR does this with a sense of community, compassion, and education, so our adopted boxers will have a lasting relationship with their forever families.

Our Values

  Carolina Boxer Rescue is guided by a set of core values and principles that shape our mission and define our commitment to the well-being of Boxer dogs. These principles include:

  • The Golden Rule: We believe in treating all living beings with the same kindness and consideration that we ourselves would wish to receive.
  • Kindness: Demonstrating compassion and respect for every living creature is at the heart of our approach to rescue and rehabilitation.
  • Positive Influence: Our effectiveness is measured by the positive impact on both the lives of the Boxers we rescue and the experiences of the people we engage with.
  • Leadership: We strive to lead by example, constantly developing, promoting, and sharing innovative ideas and programs to enhance the welfare of Boxers.
  • Authenticity: Carolina Boxer Rescue is committed to doing exactly what we say
  • Transparency: Openness and honesty in our relationships are fundamental to our operations.

  Everyone associated with Carolina Boxer Rescue is expected to embody these principles, reflecting our shared dedication to the well-being of Boxer dogs and our mission to make a positive difference in their lives.

Our Story

  Carolina Boxer Rescue was founded in October 2001 by three North Carolina women, who wanted to make a difference in the lives of shelter dogs. The work was hard, the technology of today was not present – they only had a fax machine to accept Adoption Applications! The dogs’ pictures were taken with real cameras (or polaroids!) The volunteers drove all over Virginia, North Carolina and South Carolina, pulling dogs from shelters and driving them to their foster homes. We were a small but devoted group.

  As Carolina Boxer Rescue grew, many dedicated volunteers joined the cause. We became larger, with more adoptions and foster homes than any boxer rescue in N.C. or S.C. Hundreds of dogs are saved every year by these hardworking volunteers, foster homes, and adoptive homes. Carolina Boxer Rescue is proud to have saved over 3500 dogs from euthanasia in shelters!

  Some dogs come to us too far gone to help. Some were abused, with deep scars on their physical body and in their spirit. Some were neglected. Many had extremely advanced heartworms or cancer, left untreated for years. We do the best we can for our furry friends. We give them a good life in our rescue. Many of our nubs are running free at the Rainbow Bridge. Happily, most of the time our care, love, and vet work pays off, and broken and abandoned dogs get a second chance to live the life they always dreamed about.

  Carolina Boxer Rescue is a 501(c) 3 non-profit organization, so donations are tax deductible. CBR is always in need of loving foster homes, adoptive homes, donors and volunteers. Join us in our goal to “Save the Nubs”!
