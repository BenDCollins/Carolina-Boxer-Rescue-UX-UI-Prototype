Support Us

Why Support Us?

The need for animal rescue is greater than ever, and your support fuels our mission to save the most vulnerable. At Carolina Boxer Rescue (CBR), we take pride in “leaving no nub behind,” often stepping up to care for very sick or senior boxers.

Remarkably, 80% of the boxers we rescue require high-cost medical surgeries or care, and more than half need heartworm treatment, which costs a minimum of $250 per dog. With an annual vet bill exceeding $130,000, every tax-deductible donation makes a critical difference for these wigglebutts.

Whether it’s a one-time gift or a recurring contribution, your generosity helps us create happy, healthy lives for rescued boxers and the families who love them.

Together, we can ensure every companion animal is cared for and valued, free from the fear of homelessness or neglect. Thank you for investing in saving lives and making a lasting impact!

How To Support Us?

Make a Gift

Every contribution counts! Your financial support empowers us to save lives and find families for rescued boxers. Together, we can provide the care these dogs need and ensure they find the loving homes they deserve.

Monthly Giving

The easiest way to support Carolina Boxer Rescue’s mission is through monthly giving. As a member of The Nub Club, your consistent generosity ensures we can provide the care and resources needed for every boxer that enters our program. Together, we can save lives, one nub at a time!

Donate Supplies

Have extra pet care items or toys you no longer need? We’ll take them! And check out our Amazon wish list to see what we need most.

In Honor/Memory

Celebrate the memory of a loved one or a cherished pet by making a gift to Carolina Boxer Rescue. Your tribute helps us build a brighter future for boxers in need and the families who welcome them into their homes.

Donor Advised Funds

A grant from your donor-advised fund can create lasting change for homeless boxers, helping Carolina Boxer Rescue provide care and support for years to come.

Boxer Guardians Legacy Society

Including Carolina Boxer Rescue in your estate plans is a powerful way to ensure our mission to save boxers’ lives continues for generations to come. Your legacy will provide second chances and loving homes for years into the future.

Boxer Lifeline Fund

Many of the boxers we welcome into Carolina Boxer Rescue have pre-existing medical conditions requiring specialized care, including heartworm treatments, surgeries, costly medications, diagnostic tests, and ongoing therapies. Adoption fees cover only a portion of their routine care, leaving extraordinary cases as a significant strain on our limited resources.

Giving at Work

Support the boxers of Carolina Boxer Rescue through your employer’s giving campaign by making payroll-deduction gifts. Your contributions directly help us provide the care, medical treatment, and love these deserving dogs need to thrive and find their forever homes.

Sponsor a Foster

Sponsoring a foster for CBR helps provide essential care for boxers in need while they await their forever homes. Your support covers veterinary visits, medications, food, and other necessities that ensure our foster boxers are happy and healthy. By sponsoring, you become a vital part of their journey, offering hope and love to a deserving boxer.

Shop and Save Lives

Check out our items for sale at the CBR online store. Your purchase goes toward the medical bills of our fosters.

Name a Nub

Honor or memorialize a beloved pet or friend while supporting the rescue. Choose a unique and meaningful name for one of our incoming Boxers, giving them a fresh start full of love and hope.

Shop With Our Partners

When you shop with our generous partners, they donate a portion of your purchase to Carolina Boxer Rescue. It’s an easy way to support our mission and help save lives!
